## 2. Permitted Uses (No Prior Approval Required)

You may use the WearEars wordmark without prior written permission solely for:

1. **Truthful & Nominative Reference**: Stating accurately that your software, driver, or documentation is designed for or compatible with WearEars (e.g., *"Driver for XYZ Earbuds — compatible with WearEars"*).
2. **Attribution & Origin**: Accurately attributing the original author, upstream repository, or project source as required by the MIT License copyright notice.
3. **Linking**: Linking directly to the official WearEars repository (https://github.com/hdzw1155/wearears-website) or official website (https://wearears.com).

## 3. Restricted Uses (Explicit Permission Required)

Unless you have obtained prior written consent from the project owner, you may **NOT**:

1. **Use in Product Names**: Name your commercial product, service, app, or organization "WearEars", or use a name confusingly similar to "WearEars".
2. **Official Icon & Logo Reuse**: Use the official WearEars squircle application launcher icon, geometric brand marks, or modified variants as the primary branding or icon of a fork or derivative distribution.
3. **False Endorsement or Affiliation**: Imply that your fork, project, hardware, or company is officially sponsored, endorsed, maintained, or approved by WearEars or its creators.
4. **Domain & Social Accounts**: Register domain names, social media handles, or app store publisher accounts containing "WearEars" in a manner that creates consumer confusion.

## 4. Forking & Redistribution Guidelines

If you distribute a modified version or commercial fork of this project:
* You **must** remove the official WearEars application launcher icon and replace it with your own distinct icon.
* You **must** clearly state that your distribution is a fork and is not produced, sponsored, or endorsed by the original WearEars project.
